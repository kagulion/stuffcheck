# Article Writing & Editing Guidelines

Editorial standards for writing, editing, and reviewing articles in this repository.

## Core Rules

1. **Infostyle & Conciseness ("Пиши, сокращай")**:
   - Focus on facts, practical utility, and clarity.
   - Eliminate filler words, buzzwords, канцелярит (bureaucratic jargon), and heavy nominalizations.
   - Use active verbs and clear sentence subjects.

2. **Flow & Rhythm ("Поток воды")**:
   - The prose must flow naturally, smoothly, and effortlessly.
   - Vary sentence lengths to create a dynamic reading rhythm.
   - Avoid clumsy transitions and deeply nested subordinate clauses.

3. **No Endless Lists**:
   - Default to coherent narrative paragraphs.
   - Use bullet or numbered lists only when strictly necessary (e.g., sequential guides or concise technical comparisons).

4. **Zero Emojis**:
   - Absolutely no emojis in article content, headings, notes, or assistant responses.

5. **No Parenthetical Duplicate Translations**:
   - Do not pair English terms with Russian translations in brackets/parentheses (or vice versa).
   - Use either the standard English term or the natural Russian term consistently without dubbing.

6. **Language Protocol**:
   - Instructions are in English for precise alignment.
   - All article text, edits, and conversations with the user must be in Russian.
